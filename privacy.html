Penguin Floes

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Penguin Floes is a puzzle game. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game and show ads.

What stays on your device

Your level, penguins, daily streak and settings are saved only on your device. We don’t ask for your name, email or an account.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising identifier, rough location based on your IP address, and information about how ads are viewed. On iPhone, the app asks for your permission before any tracking. You can say no and still play everything. Google’s use of data is described at policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple (or Google on Android). We never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases you own; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history in this game.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your choices

You can turn off ad tracking at any time in your phone’s settings, and you can delete all game data by deleting the app.
