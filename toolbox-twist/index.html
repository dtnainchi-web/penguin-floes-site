Toolbox Twist: Screw Puzzle

Unscrew the colourful screws, fill the matching toolboxes and watch every wooden plank drop. Relaxing, quick to learn and tricky to master.

How to play

  • Tap a screw that is not covered by another plank to unscrew it.
  • It flies into the toolbox of the same colour. A full toolbox leaves and a new one arrives.
  • If no toolbox matches, the screw waits in the spare tray. When the tray is full, the level is over.
  • Clear every plank to win. Need help? Watch a short video for one extra slot.
