Toolbox Twist

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Toolbox Twist is a screw puzzle game by Youness Nadi. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game and show ads.

What stays on your device

Your level, coins and settings are saved only on your device. We don’t ask for your name, email or an account, and the game has no servers of its own.

Ads

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a short video you can choose to watch for an extra slot, and a full-screen ad between some levels. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising identifier, approximate location based on your IP address, and information about how ads are viewed. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks for your choice first. On iPhone, the game asks your permission before any tracking. You can say no and still play everything. Google’s use of data is described at policies.google.com/privacy.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules a few reminders on your device to invite you back to play. They are created on your phone; no data is sent anywhere. You can turn them off with the bell button in the game or in your phone’s settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your choices

You can turn off ad tracking at any time in your phone’s settings, and you can delete all game data by deleting the app.
